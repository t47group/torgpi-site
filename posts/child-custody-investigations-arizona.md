When you're worried about your child's safety during the other parent's time, it's natural to want to do something. The key is doing it in a way that actually helps your case — calmly, lawfully and with documentation a court can rely on.

*This is general information, not legal advice. Work closely with your family law attorney.*

## How Arizona courts decide

Arizona courts decide legal decision-making and parenting time based on the **best interests of the child** (A.R.S. § 25-403). The statute lists factors such as the child's relationship with each parent, adjustment to home and school, the mental and physical health of those involved, and whether there has been domestic violence or substance abuse, among others.

That means the most helpful evidence is specific, factual and connected to the child's well-being — not to the parents' personal grievances.

## Evidence that tends to help

- **Supervision and safety:** whether children are left unattended or in unsafe situations
- **Substance use** around the children or before driving them
- **Who's in the home:** new partners or other adults with concerning backgrounds
- **Compliance:** whether court-ordered conditions are being followed
- **Exchange behavior:** punctuality, conduct and safety at parenting-time exchanges

## What can backfire

- **Recording conversations you aren't part of** — this can be a felony in Arizona
- **Putting a tracker on the other parent's car**
- **Going through their phone or accounts**
- **Using your child as an informant** or coaching them
- **Posting about the case on social media**

Courts notice when a parent's focus drifts from the child to the conflict.

## How an investigator helps

A licensed investigator can lawfully document conditions and conduct with time-stamped photos, video and written observations, run background checks on adults around your child, and organize findings for your attorney. Just as important, an investigator is a neutral professional — not an angry ex — which makes the findings more credible.

If a child is in immediate danger, call 911 first.
