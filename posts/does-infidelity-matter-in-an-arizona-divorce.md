If you suspect your spouse is cheating, you've probably wondered whether proof would even matter. In Arizona, the honest answer is: less than most people think for the divorce itself — and more than most people think for the things around it.

*This is general information, not legal advice. Talk with an Arizona family law attorney about your situation.*

## Arizona is (mostly) no-fault

For a standard marriage, the only ground for divorce in Arizona is that the marriage is **irretrievably broken** (A.R.S. § 25-312). You don't have to prove adultery to get divorced, and a court divides community property **without regard to marital misconduct** (A.R.S. § 25-318). Spousal maintenance decisions likewise don't turn on who cheated.

The exception is a **covenant marriage**, a less common form of marriage in Arizona where adultery is one of the specific grounds for divorce.

## Where the facts still matter

### Money spent on an affair

Courts can account for **excessive or abnormal expenditures** and wasted community funds. Hotels, gifts, trips and second phones paid for with marital money can be relevant to how property is divided. Documenting that spending is often where an investigation earns its keep.

### Hidden assets

Spouses planning to leave sometimes move money, open new accounts, or "lend" assets to friends. Asset research can help your attorney know what questions to ask in discovery.

### Cohabitation

If you're paying spousal maintenance and your former spouse may be living with a new partner, that can be relevant to modification under the terms of your decree. Cohabitation is something surveillance can document.

### Your children

Custody decisions are based on the child's best interests (A.R.S. § 25-403). An affair by itself isn't a factor, but exposing children to unsafe situations or people can be.

## And sometimes, you just need to know

Plenty of clients aren't thinking about court at all. They want the truth so they can stop second-guessing themselves and decide what's next. That's a legitimate reason, and it deserves a discreet, respectful investigation.
